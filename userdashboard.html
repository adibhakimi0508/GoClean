Report a Complaint

Report a waste or cleanliness problem in your area. Enter the site address, choose a category, describe the problem and add photos if you have any, then press Submit.



Category guide:

Domestic Waste: Issues with household garbage, such as missed collection, damaged bins or illegal dumping near homes.

Recycling: Issues with recycling services, such as full or missing recycling bins, wrongly sorted waste or collection problems.

Public Cleaning: Issues in public areas, such as litter on roads, blocked drains, parks and market areas that need cleaning.
